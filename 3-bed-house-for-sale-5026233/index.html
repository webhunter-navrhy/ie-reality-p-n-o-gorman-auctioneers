A Unique Coastal Residence On c. 4 Acres In a Truly Spectacular Setting

Flexible Living Accommodation of c. 128 sq m

Being Offered in Lots

Outstanding Refurbishment Potential

Rarely does an opportunity arise to acquire a property such as this traditional farmhouse at Ballystraw, Duncannon. Enjoying a wonderfully private setting and standing on approximately 4 acres, the property commands spectacular panoramic coastal views towards Hook Head and across to the Waterford coastline.

The farmhouse, which extends to approximately 128 sq m, retains the character and proportions while now requiring refurbishment and modernisation, it offers generous and flexible accommodation with considerable potential to create a truly special home. The adjoining outbuildings and traditional courtyard further add to the appeal and possibilities of the property. The grounds offer a wonderful sense of space and privacy with its elevated position and far-reaching sea views it is a hidden gem.

Conveniently located close to the picturesque village of Duncannon and its Blue Flag beach, the property is ideally positioned for enjoying all that The Hook Peninsula has to offer. The area is renowned for its beaches and coastal walks, together with attractions including Hook Lighthouse and Tintern Abbey. The renowned Aldridge Lodge and Dunbrody Country House Hotel are also close by, while the villages of Arthurstown, Ballyhack and Fethard-on-Sea add to the excellent choice of amenities, restaurants and places to explore throughout this special corner of South Wexford. Ballystraw represents a rare opportunity for those seeking a permanent home or coastal retreat with land, character and exceptional views, and the chance to create something entirely their own. Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate the setting and potential of this unique property.

The property is being offered as follows:

Lot 1. The Farmhouse on 0.833ha (2.05 acre)

Lot 2. Field 0.78ha (1.92 acre)

Lot 3. The Entire
